Scholarship Application Guidance & Procedures

Follow this standardized 5-step roadmap to successfully complete applications on official government and private portals without rejection.

1
Profile & DigiLocker

Keep your DigiLocker account synced with your Aadhaar, Class 10/12 marksheets, and domicile certificate. Almost all portals now use DigiLocker API for instant e-KYC.

Pro Tip: Student name spelling on Aadhaar and Marksheet MUST be identical.
2
Portal Registration

Register on National Scholarship Portal (NSP), State e-Grantz, or corporate CSR portals. Obtain your One-Time Registration (OTR) number.

Pro Tip: Use an active mobile number that is linked to your Aadhaar card for OTPs.
3
Document Upload

Upload Income Certificate (issued by Tehsildar/SDM for current financial year), Bonafide certificate signed by college principal, and fee receipts.

Pro Tip: PDF size must be under 200 KB and clearly legible.
4
Institute Verification

Submit the printed confirmation copy and fee receipts to your college/school Scholarship Nodal Officer for electronic verification on the portal.

Pro Tip: College verification deadline is usually 7 days after student submission!
5
DBT Bank Seeding

Scholarship funds are directly transferred via Aadhaar Enabled Payment System (AePS) / PFMS. Ensure your bank account is seeded with NPCI.

Pro Tip: Check your bank seeding status on the UIDAI portal under 'Check Aadhaar Bank Seeding'.
Disbursement & Renewal

Track your PFMS payment reference number. Most scholarships allow annual renewal by maintaining a minimum 60% mark in every college semester.

Scholarship Application FAQs & Rules

Essential knowledge for students applying on NSP, state e-District, and CSR portals

5 Key Guidelines

Under Government of India guidelines, a student cannot receive two Central/State government scholarships simultaneously covering the same tuition fees. However, students can combine a government scholarship with a private foundation CSR grant, merit prize, or fee concession, unless the scheme rules explicitly forbid external financial aid.

The OTR is a unique 14-digit identifier generated via Aadhaar biometric or face authentication. Once created, it stays with the applicant through School, Diploma, Undergraduate, and Postgraduate studies, eliminating redundant annual registrations.

Simply opening a bank account is not enough for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). You must submit an "Aadhaar NPCI Mapping Form" to your bank branch. The bank maps your Aadhaar number in the NPCI server to enable automatic scholarship disbursement via PFMS.

Top 4 Rejection Traps
1. Expired Financial Year Income Proof

Income certificates must be issued after April 1st of the current fiscal year. Older certificates lead to immediate rejection.

2. Mismatched Name in Bank Account

Account must be in student's name, not joint account with parent (unless explicitly allowed), and linked with Aadhaar NPCI mapper.

3. Missing Stamp on Bonafide

Every bonafide certificate must have the college round seal and registrar's signature with current academic year mentioned.

4. Unofficial Online Marksheets

Provisional marks printed from the web must be physically counter-signed and stamped by the college principal.
